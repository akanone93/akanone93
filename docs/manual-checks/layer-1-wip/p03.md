- **Needs:** R-STACK; an Android 7.0 (API 24) emulator or phone
- **Do:**
  1. `grep -n minSdk packages/plux_flutter/android/build.gradle.kts`; `grep -n platform packages/plux_flutter/ios/plux_flutter.podspec`
